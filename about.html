Soundsation

Soundsation was a UK electronic music project active between 1994 and 1999, known for releases including “Do You Feel It”, “Peace & Joy”, and “New Direction”.

The project achieved UK chart entries, BBC Radio 1 support, national music press coverage, and sustained DJ play within the progressive house scene.

Soundsation’s releases circulated within the UK club system and were supported by DJs including John Digweed and Judge Jules, with coverage in DJ Magazine and Muzik.

About Soundsation

Soundsation was a UK electronic music project active between 1994 and 1999, associated with the progressive house scene. The project received BBC Radio 1 support and entered both the UK Dance Singles Chart and UK Singles Chart.

Soundsation is sometimes credited as Soundstation in contemporary press and chart listings.

  • Do You Feel It – UK Dance Chart entry, DJ Magazine and Muzik coverage
  • Peace & Joy – UK Singles Chart entry
  • New Direction – BBC Radio 1 support (Judge Jules, 1998)
